Questions

Frequently Asked

Is my donation tax-deductible?
Yes. Messianic Movement is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. You will receive an instant email receipt and an annual tax statement every January for your records.
Can I designate my gift to a specific project?
Absolutely. Use the "Choose Your Field" cards above to direct your gift to Tithes, Missions, Building, Divine Episodes, Youth, or Relief. If you select "Tithes & Offerings," your gift will be used where most needed.
How do I update or cancel a recurring gift?
You can manage your recurring gifts at any time through your Partner Portal. Simply click "Manage Giving" in your receipt email, or contact our Finance Team at finance@whgrministry.org and we will assist within 24 hours.
What percentage goes to administrative costs?
We maintain a lean 12% operational overhead. That means 88 cents of every dollar goes directly to ministry—missions, media production, building projects, and compassion outreach. Our financials are audited annually and published on our Transparency page.
Can I give by check, wire, or stock transfer?
Yes. We accept all forms of giving. For check, mail to: WHGRM Finance, 123 Faith Avenue, Glory City, GC 10001. For wire transfers and stock donations, email finance@whgrministry.org and we will send secure instructions within one business day.
