Vision & Mission

We exist to raise a generation of Spirit-filled believers who will transform nations through the power of the Holy Ghost.

Our Vision

To see a global army of regenerated men and women walking in the fullness of the Holy Spirit, demonstrating the power of God in every sphere of society. We envision churches planted in every nation, communities transformed by the Gospel, and a generation that knows no limits in the supernatural.

Our Mission

To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ with signs and wonders, to disciple believers into maturity through the Word and the Spirit, to equip ministers for end-time harvest, and to demonstrate compassion to the poor and oppressed. We are committed to preparing the world for the messianic era.

Founder & Senior Pastor

Pastor John James

Pastor John James is the visionary founder and Senior Pastor of Messianic Movement. Born in Lagos, Nigeria, he received a powerful calling to ministry at the age of 19 after a life-changing encounter with the Holy Ghost during a prayer retreat.

With over 25 years of apostolic ministry, Pastor John has pioneered a movement that spans over 50 nations. His teachings on divine regeneration, the baptism of the Holy Spirit, and apostolic authority have transformed countless lives worldwide.

He holds a Doctorate in Theology and is a respected voice in the global Pentecostal community. Under his leadership, WHGRM has grown from a small prayer group to a global network of churches, Bible schools, and humanitarian organizations.

"The church is not a building. It is a people—a burning, shining generation that carries the fire of God into every dark place. We were born for such a time as this."

— Pastor John James
Co-Senior Pastor

Pastor Mrs. Joice James

Pastor Mrs. Joice James serves as Co-Senior Pastor alongside her husband, bringing a powerful dimension of grace, wisdom, and prophetic insight to the ministry. A woman of deep prayer and uncompromising faith, she leads the women's ministry and oversees the pastoral care department.

With a background in counseling and ministry training, Pastor Joice has developed programs that have restored marriages, healed broken families, and raised up women leaders across Africa and beyond. Her annual "Women of Fire" conference draws thousands of women seeking spiritual empowerment.

She is also the founder of the Compassion Works initiative, which has provided food, education, and medical care to over 100,000 vulnerable children and families across sub-Saharan Africa.

"Every woman is a vessel of fire. When a woman catches the flame of the Holy Ghost, she doesn't just burn—she ignites nations."

— Pastor Mrs. Joice James

Our Journey

History of the Ministry

From a small prayer gathering to a global movement. Every milestone is a testimony of God's faithfulness.

2001

The Birth of a Vision

Pastor John James receives a divine mandate during a 40-day fast. A small prayer group of 12 people gathers in his living room in Lagos, Nigeria. The first service is held under a mango tree with plastic chairs and a heart full of faith.

2005

First Permanent Sanctuary

After four years of explosive growth, the ministry acquires its first property—a 500-seat auditorium on Faith Avenue. The dedication service sees over 1,200 people in attendance, with miracles, healings, and mass baptisms in the Holy Spirit.

2008

Global Expansion Begins

The first international branch is planted in Accra, Ghana. Within the same year, branches open in London, Toronto, and Johannesburg. The ministry officially adopts the name Messianic Movement.

2012

School of the Spirit Launched

The Bible training institute opens its doors, offering systematic theological education combined with hands-on ministry training. Over 500 students enroll in the first year. Today, the school has graduated over 10,000 ministers.

2015

Compassion Works Initiative

Pastor Mrs. Joice James launches the humanitarian arm of the ministry. The first project provides clean water to 50 villages in rural Nigeria. Today, Compassion Works operates in 15 countries.

2018

The New Worship Center

Construction begins on the 15,000-seat Glory Dome—a state-of-the-art worship center designed to host international conferences, crusades, and gatherings. The project is completed debt-free in 2020.

2023

50 Nations & Counting

The ministry celebrates reaching 50 nations with the Gospel. Over 200 churches, 50 Bible schools, and countless humanitarian projects now operate under the WHGRM banner. The vision continues to expand.

2026

The Era of Regeneration

Today, WHGRM stands as a global force for the Gospel. With over 15,000 members at the flagship assembly and millions reached through media, the ministry continues to blaze forward—raising men in the power of the Spirit.

What We Believe

Statement of Faith

These are the foundational truths that guide our doctrine, teaching, and practice as a ministry.

1

The Trinity

We believe in one God, eternally existent in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—co-equal, co-eternal, and co-existent.

2

The Bible

We believe the Holy Scriptures, both Old and New Testaments, are the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God.

3

Jesus Christ

We believe in the deity of Jesus Christ, His virgin birth, sinless life, miracles, atoning death, bodily resurrection, and His personal return in power and glory.

4

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ. It is a free gift that cannot be earned, only received through repentance and confession.

5

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit, the manifestation of spiritual gifts, and the ongoing work of the Spirit in regeneration, empowerment, and guidance.

6

Divine Healing

We believe in divine healing for the body, soul, and spirit through the atoning work of Christ. Healing is the children's bread and available to all believers.

7

The Church

We believe the Church is the Body of Christ, called to worship, fellowship, discipleship, ministry, and evangelism until Christ returns.

8

The Second Coming

We believe in the literal, personal, and imminent return of Jesus Christ to establish His kingdom, judge the nations, and dwell with His people forever.
