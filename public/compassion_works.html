Our Mandate

Faith in Action

As crises multiply across the earth — from armed conflicts and mass displacement to natural disasters and famine — Compassion Works remains a cornerstone of WHGRM's global mission. Over two decades of frontline engagement, we have built vast expertise in emergency relief, medical missions, and spiritual trauma care — often in the most challenging and hostile environments.


This experience equips us to extend our capacity to support emergency preparedness and long-term development efforts, depending on the local context. Working closely with local churches, government institutions, and international partners, we make our assets and functionalities available to fellow humanitarian operators, allowing them to save more lives, faster, and at lower cost — all while sharing the Gospel of regeneration.

What We Do

Services & Operations

Comprehensive humanitarian and spiritual support delivered on behalf of churches, NGOs, and government institutions worldwide.

Emergency Relief & Disaster Response

Rapid deployment teams responding within 24–72 hours to natural disasters, conflicts, and humanitarian crises. We deliver food, clean water, temporary shelter, and emergency supplies to affected populations.

Learn More

Medical Missions & Wellness

Mobile clinics, surgical missions, and wellness programs serving underserved communities. From rural Africa to refugee camps in the Middle East, our medical teams bring healing in Jesus' name.

Learn More

Education & Bible Distribution

The World Bible Education Project — disseminating divine knowledge to all nations. We distribute Scriptures, establish learning centers, and train local leaders in biblical literacy and discipleship.

Learn More

Food Security & Nutrition

Procurement and distribution of nutritious food for children, families, and vulnerable groups. We operate feeding programs, agricultural training, and sustainable food systems in food-insecure regions.

Learn More

Shelter & Reconstruction

Engineering support for rebuilding homes, schools, and places of worship destroyed by disaster or war. We provide temporary housing, mobile warehouses, and construction expertise.

Learn More

Spiritual Counseling & Trauma Care

Trained counselors and ministers providing trauma-informed spiritual care to victims of war, abuse, and displacement. We believe true healing comes through the regenerating power of the Holy Ghost.
