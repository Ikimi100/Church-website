Our Mission

Dimensions of Response

We deploy spiritual and humanitarian aid wherever believers are persecuted, displaced, or in desperate need. Our work is guided by the mandate to be the hands and feet of Christ.

Persecution Response

Standing with believers facing imprisonment, violence, and discrimination for their faith. We provide legal aid, safe housing, and spiritual support to the persecuted church worldwide.

Crisis Relief

Rapid deployment of food, medical supplies, and shelter to communities devastated by war, natural disaster, and displacement. Bringing hope to the hopeless in their darkest hour.

Spiritual Aid

Delivering Bibles, discipleship materials, and pastoral training to underground churches and restricted nations. Equipping the saints with the Word in the midst of fire.

Family Restoration

Reconnecting displaced families, rescuing children from trafficking, and establishing safe houses for widows and orphans. Restoring what the enemy has stolen.
