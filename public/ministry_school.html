Our Mandate

Raising Spirit-Filled Leaders for Global Impact

The Ministry School of the Spirit is a divine training ground where believers are transformed into vessels of honor, equipped with the uncompromised Word and the demonstration of the Holy Ghost's power.

Founded on the revelation of regeneration and the messianic mandate, our school exists to prepare men and women who will stand as divine messengers in this generation—carrying the fire of revival to every nation.

  • Comprehensive biblical theology rooted in Messianic understanding
  • Hands-on ministry training with spiritual formation
  • Global mission exposure and cross-cultural engagement
  • Leadership development for church planting and revival

Course Catalog

Core Curriculum

A comprehensive selection of courses designed to equip you in every dimension of ministry.

01

Introduction to the Scriptures

A foundational overview of the Bible's structure, themes, and the progressive revelation of God's redemptive plan from Genesis to Revelation.

02

Biblical Hermeneutics

The art and science of biblical interpretation—learning to rightly divide the Word of truth through historical, grammatical, and contextual analysis.

03

Doctrine of the Trinity

Exploring the nature of God as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—understanding the Godhead and its implications for worship and ministry.

04

Christology & Soteriology

The person and work of Jesus Christ, and the doctrine of salvation—regeneration, justification, sanctification, and glorification.

05

Pneumatology & the Holy Ghost

The doctrine of the Holy Spirit—His person, work, gifts, and the believer's relationship with the Spirit of power and regeneration.

06

Church History & Reformation

Tracing the history of the Church from Pentecost to the present, with special focus on the Reformation and revivals that shaped our faith.

07

Leadership & Ministry Management

Biblical principles of leadership, church administration, team building, and the practical aspects of leading a Spirit-filled ministry.

08

Christian Ethics & Marriage

Navigating moral and ethical challenges in ministry, building godly homes, and understanding biblical standards for Christian living.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know about enrolling in the Ministry School of the Spirit.

What are the admission requirements?

Applicants must be born-again believers with a genuine call to ministry. A high school diploma or equivalent is required for the Diploma and Advanced Diploma programs. All applicants undergo a pastoral reference check and a brief interview to confirm their readiness for the program.

Can I study while working full-time?

Absolutely. We offer flexible learning options including weekend intensives (Friday evenings and Saturdays) and evening classes (Sunday through Tuesday, 5pm–9:30pm). Many of our students successfully balance work, family, and ministry while completing their studies.

Are the classes available online?

Yes, we offer a hybrid learning model. While we encourage physical attendance for the full impartation experience, all core lectures are live-streamed and recorded for remote students. Practical ministry components require in-person participation.

What payment options are available?

We accept bank transfers, card payments at the ministry office, and structured payment plans. A non-refundable application fee of $50 is due with your application. Tuition can be paid in full or in semester installments. Scholarships are available for deserving students.

Will I be ordained after graduation?

Advanced Diploma graduates who complete the ordination track, including the apostolic internship and mission practicum, are eligible for ministerial ordination through Messianic Movement. Diploma graduates receive ministerial licensing.
