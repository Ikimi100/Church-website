Frequently Asked

Is Divine Episodes completely free?
Yes. Every episode, course, and study material is offered freely as part of our mandate to disseminate divine knowledge to all nations. No subscription required.
Can I watch past episodes?
Absolutely. Our full archive of 312+ episodes is available on-demand. Browse by book, theme, or teacher to find exactly what you need.
Are there certificates for completing courses?
Yes. Students who complete a full course series with all session quizzes receive a digital Certificate of Biblical Studies from WHGRM Ministry School.
How do I join the live Sunday broadcast?
Divine Episodes airs live every Sunday at 9:00 AM EST on our Live page, YouTube, and Facebook. You can also set a reminder via the notification bell.
