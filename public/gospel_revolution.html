Our Mandate

Raising an Army of Spirit-Filled Evangelists

Gospel Revolution is not merely an outreach program — it is a divine mobilization. We believe that every believer is called to be a witness, and we exist to equip, train, and deploy men and women who carry the fire of the Holy Ghost into every sphere of society.

Our approach combines aggressive evangelism with compassionate humanitarian aid. We go where others won't, speak what others fear to say, and demonstrate the kingdom through signs, wonders, and radical love.

  • Mass crusades and open-air evangelism in unreached regions
  • Church planting movements in persecuted and neglected areas
  • Media evangelism reaching millions through digital platforms
  • Humanitarian missions — feeding the hungry and healing the sick
  • Training schools raising indigenous missionaries and pastors
