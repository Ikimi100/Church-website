Questions

Frequently Asked

What is the best season for a Holy Land pilgrimage?
Spring (March–May) and Fall (September–November) offer the most pleasant weather and vibrant landscapes. However, we operate year-round. Winter pilgrimages offer quieter sites and a unique atmosphere, while summer tours include more indoor teaching and evening activities.
Is it safe to travel to Israel and Jordan?
As a ministry, we take a biblical perspective on peace in the region. We do not believe lasting peace will come until the Prince of Peace returns. Our expert drivers and guides carefully avoid any areas of concern and treat you as our honored guest. We have safely led 500+ pilgrims without incident.
Can I be baptized in the Jordan River?
Yes. Yardenit baptism site is included in most itineraries. We provide white garments, changing facilities, and a meaningful ceremony led by your tour pastor. It is one of the most powerful moments of the entire pilgrimage for many travelers.
What is the physical requirement for these tours?
Our tours require walking approximately 3–4 miles per day on average, often across uneven terrain and hills. Comfortable, supportive footwear is essential. At each site, our guides offer options for those with physical limitations. If you have concerns, contact us for a private consultation.
Do you offer private group tours for churches?
Absolutely. If your church or ministry group has 10 or more people, we can customize an itinerary to fit your specific needs, teaching focus, and schedule. Private tours include dedicated worship sessions and tailored teaching from your own leadership.
What should I pack and wear?
Dress modestly with shoulders and knees covered at religious sites. Men should bring a head covering for the Western Wall (we provide one if needed). Bring comfortable walking shoes, a hat, sunscreen, and a reusable water bottle. A detailed packing list is sent upon registration.
